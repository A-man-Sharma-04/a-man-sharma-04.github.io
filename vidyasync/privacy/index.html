VidyaSync Privacy Policy

Effective date:

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VidyaSync ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using VidyaSync, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account identifier for the purpose of creating and managing your account.
  • School and family data: Student names, parent/guardian contact details, school records, and bus route information provided by your school.
  • Location and telemetry: For drivers of school buses, we collect vehicle location data while the app is being used to operate the bus route. Parents may view the bus location.
  • Usage data: App interaction data to improve functionality and reliability.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate and secure the VidyaSync platform.
  • To enable live bus tracking, pickup/drop, and attendance features.
  • To send important notifications about your child's school commute and attendance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Google Sign-In

VidyaSync uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in with Google, we receive only the basic profile information you have authorized (such as your name, email address, and unique Google identifier) needed to create and identify your account. We do not publish or share your Google account information with third parties.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with your school or organization (to operate the service) and with service providers that help us run the platform, under confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including strict multi-tenant isolation, encryption in transit, and access controls.

7. Location Permissions

Location data for bus drivers is collected only while actively operating a route and is used solely for live bus tracking. Parents use the app to view bus location; we do not collect the parent's own location.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may also sign out and revoke Google account access at any time through your Google account settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@vidyasync.com.
