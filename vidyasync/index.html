About VidyaSync

VidyaSync is a school management and live bus-tracking platform that connects parents, teachers, drivers and school administrators in one secure application. It provides real-time school bus location, student pickup/drop management, attendance, and daily communication between schools and families.

Key Features

  • Live bus tracking - real-time vehicle location with routes and stop points.
  • Smart attendance engine - automatically marks bus-delayed students as present in bus.
  • Role-based access - dedicated experiences for parents, teachers, drivers and administrators.
  • Strict multi-tenancy - every school's data is fully isolated and secure.
  • Push notifications - instant alerts for pickup/drop and attendance events.

For Parents

Track your child's school bus in real time, see live pickup/drop status, and receive notifications about arrival and attendance.

For Schools

Manage routes, drivers, buses, student records and attendance across your entire campus from one dashboard.
